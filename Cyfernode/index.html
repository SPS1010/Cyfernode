How a mission works

From idea to liftoff,
in four steps.

Every project on GitHub is now a Mission: a goal, a crew and a launch date. Here's the flight path.

01 · Plan

Plan with Ground Control

Describe your idea in plain words. Ground Control turns it into a mission plan you can actually follow.

02 · Build

Build with your crew

Write code with AI at your side. Every change is saved to the Flight Log, so nothing is ever lost.

03 · Dock

Dock safely

Crew work arrives as a docking request. Ground Control checks it, explains it, and it docks. Safely.

04 · Launch

Launch to the world

One button, one countdown. Your mission gets a live link you can share with anyone.

Ground Control · Your AI crew

An AI crew that explains everything.

Ground Control plans the mission, writes code with you and directs AI agents on your behalf. And when something looks scary, it tells you what's happening in plain English.

  • Plans the missionTurns "I want to make..." into clear steps, in order.
  • Builds with youWrites code alongside you and shows you why it works.
  • Directs AI agentsHand off tasks to agents, watch them work, approve what docks.
  • Speaks plain EnglishEvery error, every term, translated. It explains, never gatekeeps.
